## Project name

Forever Pathways

## Summary (one line, shown in search results)

A ring-shaped skill tree on ProgressiveStages: earn Focal Shards, break the Seals and unlock the game age by age.

## Description (paste into the description editor; it accepts Markdown)

---

**You wake with a flint knife and a glowing book. Almost everything else is locked inside the ring.**

Forever Pathways turns progression into a skill tree you can see. Over 250 nodes sit on 16 rings, from
the Stone Age at the centre to the Eye Ring at the edge. Each node unlocks a set of tools, blocks or
machines. You pay for nodes with **Focal Shards**, and shards are earned, never found.

### How you earn

- **Tasks.** Most nodes carry a one-off task: smelt 48 copper, walk 6,000 blocks, kill 40 skeletons.
  Finish it and the node is yours to buy.
- **Bounties.** Two timed jobs every dawn, drawn from what you have unlocked. Small, steady, and most
  of your early income.
- **Discoveries.** A handful of vanilla advancements pay once.

### How it holds you back

- **Seals** stand between the ages. Each needs a Pathway Level and one large task.
- **Death** costs a shard, a quarter of every running task, and a day's freeze.
- **Your body grows with you.** Movement, breaking, attack and health start low and rise with perk nodes.

### Locks that make sense

Locked tools and items cannot be used. Locked machines cannot be opened, placed or broken. Plain blocks,
doors, gates, buttons and levers always work, and decoration only locks its recipe. Bosses cannot be
hurt until the node that asks for them is open, so no kill is ever wasted.

### The Forever Grimoire

With Patchouli installed, every player spawns with the Grimoire: how to survive the first night, how
the ring works, where every shard comes from, how to find the sixteen eyes, and an appendix for each
supported mod. Progress popups and completion banners keep you posted while you play, and each player
can switch them off.

### Works with your pack

The tree can be finished on vanilla plus ProgressiveStages alone. Every supported mod adds to it:
Create, Power Grid, Iron's Spellbooks, Supplementaries, Handcrafted, Farmer's Delight, Butchery, It's
Harder Early, Waystones, Envelope, Chalk, Sleep Tight, Icy's Better Horses, Hardcore Torches, Advanced
Chimneys and more. Missing mods simply leave their nodes as shards-only steps.

### Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge
- **ProgressiveStages** (required)
- Patchouli and Legendary Tabs recommended
- Install on the server **and** every client
